### Repair (b) acceptance

A regression-first implementation passes only when all of these hold:

- a synthetic positive fixture matching the confirmed sanitized shape links one producer to exactly one handler;
- the relation is carried through `FeatureCandidateBuilder` instead of being dropped before knowledge synthesis;
- rendered flow labels the route as queued/deferred, not synchronous execution;
- downstream product-feature flow receives a usable producer → handler edge;
- missing consumer, mismatched identity, unsupported resolver, ambiguous handler or duplicate callable fact emits no guessed concrete handler;
- known producer-side identity with an unproven destination remains explicitly unresolved;
- existing `invokes`, direct DI `dispatches`, sole-implementation dispatch and existing unresolved-dispatch behavior remain unchanged;
- recurring schedule semantics, retry/delivery guarantees, timing and business intent are not inferred.
