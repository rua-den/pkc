## Rereview-9 blocker disposition

Rereview 9 showed that exact `System.Linq.Queryable.<operation>` identity cannot by itself prove Product Owner semantics because runtime execution is delegated to `IQueryProvider`.

Production `c310e893...` closes that gap conservatively in two places:

1. a predicate invocation whose semantic containing type is exactly `System.Linq.Queryable` receives no observable Product Owner context, so the fact becomes `businessRuleAuthority=observed-only`;
2. an otherwise-authoritative Enumerable `Where` path loses returned-item authority as soon as any later pipeline invocation resolves to `System.Linq.Queryable.*`.

The downgrade preserves the predicate fact. `contains-condition` becomes `observes-predicate`, and `FeatureCandidateBuilder` now traverses `observes-predicate` as behavior evidence.

This is generic. No PokeTrade, Loren, Jellyfin, EF, provider-brand, entity-name, property-name or fixture-text exception exists.

### Findings

- Direct `Queryable` predicates fail closed before observable-return-path proof.
- Static syntax does not bypass semantic containing-type detection; unsupported/static pipeline shapes are conservative false negatives rather than false authority.
- Ambiguous semantic resolution does not produce authority.
- Any Queryable hop in the supported returned `Where` pipeline is rejected before the old safe-operation allowlists or projection proof can preserve authority.
- Pure LINQ-to-Objects filtering remains authoritative where the existing exact-shape proof applies.
- `GroundedKnowledgeSynthesizer.BuildRules()` reads business predicates only through `contains-condition`; downgraded `observes-predicate` relations therefore do not become workflow rules.
- `EvidenceAwareKnowledgeSynthesizer` does not re-promote business-predicate Evidence into rules.
- `ProductFeatureBuilder` aggregates `FeatureKnowledge.Rules`, not Evidence, so product features do not re-promote the downgraded predicate.
- Portable workflow Markdown renders rules under `Observed business rules` and lower-authority facts under a separate `Evidence` section. AI instructions explicitly warn not to upgrade incomplete evidence into stronger claims.

The rereview-9 custom-provider counterexample is therefore closed.
